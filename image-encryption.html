How to encrypt/decrypt your image:

Choose a PNG image, select a password, and click Hide Secret Image. Crypta encrypts it with AES-GCM and creates one viewable QR PNG image, even for large files. To decrypt, upload that QR PNG, enter the same password, and click Reveal Secret Image. Processing stays local to this browser.

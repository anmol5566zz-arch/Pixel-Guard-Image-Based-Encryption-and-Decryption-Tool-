How it works:

This tool uses a symmetric XOR pixel algorithm. Your password generates a unique mathematical stream that scrambles the RGB values of every pixel. To decrypt, you must upload the EXACT encrypted PNG and use the EXACT same password. Note: Encrypted images are always saved as PNGs. Saving as JPEG will compress the image and permanently destroy the encrypted data.
